How this measurement works, and where it fails

Method. PERCLOS is the measure used in driver-drowsiness research and in deployed vehicle safety systems; eye aspect ratio follows Soukupová & Čech (2016).

Limits. Spectacles with strong reflections break iris tracking. Gaze here is a direction estimate, not a calibrated screen-coordinate eye tracker.

All processing runs in your browser. No video frame is uploaded or stored anywhere.
