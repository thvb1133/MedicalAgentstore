The acoustic measures describe the sound of a voice and nothing more. Published links between them and depression, Parkinson's or cognitive decline are population-level findings that do not transfer to one person in one conversation, and the assistant is instructed never to apply them. It is not a therapist and cannot diagnose anything.

How this measurement works, and where it fails

Method. Combines the rPPG chain used by the vitals agent with the standard clinical-phonetics measures — F0, jitter, shimmer and harmonics-to-noise ratio as defined by Boersma (1993) and implemented in Praat. The conversation layer is Claude, given the measurements as sensor context with its limits stated in the system prompt.

Limits. The acoustic measures describe the sound of a voice and nothing more. Published links between them and depression, Parkinson's or cognitive decline are population-level findings that do not transfer to one person in one conversation, and the assistant is instructed never to apply them. It is not a therapist and cannot diagnose anything.

All processing runs in your browser. No video frame is uploaded or stored anywhere.
