Your history

A single webcam heart rate is a curiosity. The same measurement taken repeatedly, compared against your own earlier readings, is the part that could actually tell you something — and comparing you to yourself is far safer ground than comparing you to a population.

What Claude makes of it

Reads your last few readings as a sequence. It is told to say which comparisons the signal quality does not support, and it cannot name a condition.

Needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. The measurements and trends above work without it.

Every reading

Readings are kept in this browser, and mirrored to your S3 bucket only if one is configured. Neither store ever holds a frame of video or a second of audio — only the numbers you can see here.
